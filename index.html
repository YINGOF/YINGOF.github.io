01 / About me

Researching how machines
understand visual scenes.

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). My research interests focus on computer vision, geometric scene understanding, and robust image restoration. I am particularly interested in extracting reliable structure from RGB images and improving perception under challenging visual conditions.

02 / Research

Areas of interest.

My current research spans geometric inference, image quality enhancement, and practical scene perception.

01

Monocular Depth Estimation

Inferring dense scene depth from a single RGB image, including diffusion-based approaches to depth prediction.

02

Low-Light Image Enhancement

Studying how spatial relationships and geometric priors can support image restoration in severely underexposed scenes.

03

Road-Scene Perception

Investigating efficient visual perception for road environments, including object detection and localized obstacle understanding.
